# Welcome to Velfi

Tell your money what to do.

**Velfi is an AI money agent.** You tell it what you want in plain language — "split rent three ways," "pay my designer every Friday," "hold this until the work ships" — and Velfi builds the payment and runs it. One sentence in, money moves.

No seed phrases. No gas tokens. No wallet addresses to copy. You sign in with Google, claim a name like `alice.vel`, and start instructing your money.

### What you can do

* **Send** money instantly to a `.vel` name — or to an email address the recipient claims later.
* **Split** a payment across many people, each with their own share.
* **Stream** a salary or payout continuously over time (coming soon).
* **Schedule** recurring payments on any cadence.
* **Escrow** funds until a condition is met (coming soon).
* **Automate** rules like saving a percentage of everything you receive (coming soon).

### Launching on BNB Chain

Velfi is launching on BNB Chain, so payments settle almost instantly for a fraction of a cent, with stablecoins like USDC built in. But you never have to think about any of that — the chain is invisible. Your money just does what you tell it to.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Your wallet stays yours.** Velfi is non-custodial. It builds and proposes your payments, but only you can approve them.
{% endhint %}
