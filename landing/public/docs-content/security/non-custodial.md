# Non-custodial by design

Velfi never holds your money. This is one of the most important things to understand about how it works.

### What "non-custodial" means

A **custodial** service holds your funds for you — like a bank, or most crypto exchanges. You trust them to keep your money safe and give it back when you ask.

Velfi is **non-custodial**. Your money stays in your own wallet. Velfi can build and propose payments, but money only leaves your wallet with your approval.

### Why it matters

* **No one can freeze or seize your balance.** There's no company account holding your money.
* **Velfi can't lose your funds.** They're not in Velfi's possession to lose.
* **You're always in control.** Every payment requires your explicit approval.

### Two things you approve once

* **Scheduled payments.** When you set one up, you sign once to let Velfi's Autopilot spend up to that schedule's total from your wallet. The token contract on BNB Chain enforces that total. Velfi's server pays only the person, amount and timetable you approved, and cancelling in Flows stops it and removes the permission. The money stays in your wallet until each payment.
* **Sending to an email.** If the person isn't on Velfi yet, the money waits in a one-off holding wallet Velfi manages, for up to 7 days. It moves to their wallet when they sign up, or comes back to you automatically.

### The AI can't spend your money

Velfi's AI agent reads your instructions and proposes payments — but it has no ability to move money on its own. It has no keys and no spending power. The worst a mistaken or misbehaving agent could do is suggest a payment, which you would simply reject.

{% hint style="success" %}
**The rule is simple:** the agent proposes, you approve, and your wallet stays yours. Money never moves without your approval.
{% endhint %}

### Every payment is verifiable

Because payments settle on BNB Chain, every transaction is recorded publicly and can be independently verified on BscScan. Your payment history doesn't depend on trusting Velfi's servers.
