# FAQ

### Is Velfi custodial?

No. Velfi is non-custodial by design: your money stays in your own wallet and every payment needs your approval. The one exception is money sent to an email that isn't on Velfi yet, which waits in a holding wallet Velfi manages for up to 7 days. Read more in [Non-custodial by design](/velfi/security/non-custodial.md).

### What does a .vel name cost?

Your `.vel` name is free to claim during early access. It replaces wallet addresses for you and anyone who pays you.

### Which blockchain does Velfi use?

Velfi is launching on **Solana**, chosen for its near-instant settlement, fees of a fraction of a cent, and deep stablecoin liquidity. More networks may be supported over time, but Solana is our home.

### Is my money safe?

Velfi is designed so nothing moves without your approval. Your funds stay in your own wallet, you sign in without a seed phrase, payments run on Solana, and every transaction is recorded on-chain and verifiable. See [Security](/velfi/security/non-custodial.md).

### Do I need crypto experience?

Not at all. You sign in with Google, claim a name, and tell Velfi what you want in plain language. There are no wallets, seed phrases, or gas tokens to manage.

### Can the AI spend my money?

No. The AI agent only reads your instructions and proposes payments. It has no keys and cannot move funds on its own. Nothing happens until you approve it.

### What happens if I lose my phone?

Nothing is lost. Because your wallet comes from your Google sign-in, you simply sign in again on a new device and your wallet and balance return.

### Can I send money to someone who isn't on Velfi?

Yes. You can send to an email address, and the recipient claims the funds when they sign up. See [Send to an email](/velfi/guides/email-to-claim.md).

### Can I cancel a payment?

One-time payments settle instantly and can't be reversed once approved, so check details before confirming. Unclaimed email payments come back to you automatically after 7 days. Scheduled payments can be cancelled anytime in Flows.

### When does Velfi launch?

You can join the waitlist now and claim your name. The app is coming soon — waitlist members get early access first.

### Does Velfi have a token?

Yes. $VELFI is launching on Solana with a total supply of 1,000,000,000 and a fair launch. The contract address isn't live yet, so only trust the one posted in the docs and on [@velfi\_xyz](https://x.com/velfi_xyz). See [$VELFI token](/velfi/token/velfi.md).
